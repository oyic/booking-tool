# Design & Brand Guidelines – Lektorat Mac

## Principles
- **Mobile-first:** over 70% of users are mobile.
- **Accessibility:** WCAG AA minimum.

## Layout & Spacing
- Grid system with 8pt spacing.
- Consistent vertical rhythm.
- Max line length: 60–75 characters.

## Typography
- H1: 28–32px, H2: 22–24px, Body: 16–18px.
- Clear hierarchy, strong contrast.

## Colors
- Academic, professional palette.
- High contrast for readability.
- Highlight animations in subtle brand accent color.

## Animations
- Highlight key words (150–250ms ease).
- Sequential bullet point reveals.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Components
- Buttons: clear states (normal, hover, disabled, loading).
- Forms: labeled inputs, inline validation, accessible errors.

## Assets
- SVG where possible.
- Lazy-load heavy images.
- Optimize for fast performance.
